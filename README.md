QJams — Your music. Your practice. Your results.

Bring backing tracks, multi-track recording, synchronized video, editing, 
playlists and practice sessions together in one focused Linux application.

Build a backing track in seconds or complete composition or practice session
in minutes. Record yourself with synchronized video. Switch inputs while 
recording. Adjust levels while you play. Slow down your recordings to analyze
your playing. Stack your tracks safely with automatic clipping prevention. 
Edit what you need without getting buried in a full-sized DAW.

I designed QJams around one core idea: start playing quickly or create a 
full composition quickly, and keep the software out of the way. With its
unique, lightning-fast workflow, QJams is unlike any traditional DAW. 

Built for guitarists. Ready for musicians. Made for Linux. Developed in C.
